## What the €100 X-Perps voucher is actually worth

It is not €100 of credit. The voucher opens a €100 position using €20 of margin at 5x leverage, and you receive only the profit that position generates. The €20 margin itself is not paid out, and if the position closes at a loss you get nothing. At the page's conversion rate of 1 EUR = 1.15 USDC, the voucher displays as 115 USDC.

Read that as a small leveraged trade with capped downside for you and no guaranteed upside: it can produce a real payout if the position ends in profit, and it can produce exactly zero. It cannot be withdrawn as cash, and it does not reduce the deposit you need for the other tiers. Anyone comparing the €200 headline against a competitor's cash bonus should discount this line entirely and compare the five cash milestones instead.

## Four rules that decide whether the reward survives

### 1. Net deposit, not deposit

OKX measures **Net Deposit = Qualifying Deposits − Qualifying Withdrawals**. Deposit €10,000 and later withdraw €2,000 and your net deposit is €8,000, not €10,000. Receiving a €1,000 internal transfer from another OKX user does not push it back up to €9,000.

Internal transfers, transfers between your own accounts or sub-accounts, failed or reversed or recalled deposits, and funds that arrived through Crypto Gift or another promotional reward all fail to qualify. If your net deposit drops below the required amount before the task is verified, or before a holding period ends, you may stop satisfying the task. If you plan to move money around during the campaign week, leave a buffer above the threshold. 👉 [Check your net-deposit requirement in the app after signing up](https://okx.com/join/CASH20)

### 2. Volume that doesn't count

Trades on 0-fee pairs and stablecoin pairs are excluded, with the terms naming USDT/USD, USDT/USDC, USDT/USDG, PYUSD/USDT and RLUSD/USDT. Zero mark-up buy, sell or convert orders are excluded too, including USDC/USDT, USDC/USDG, USDC/USD and USDG/USD. The EEA version of the terms also excludes the USDC/EUR spot pair.

The practical consequence: the cheap route of moving EUR into USDC and calling it volume does nothing for you. Every euro of qualifying volume has to go through a non-stablecoin pair, which means you are holding price exposure while you generate it. That is the real cost of the upper tiers, not just the fee line.

### 3. Lock-ups: 45 days, 90 days, 30 days

Crypto rewards are locked for a qualifying period of up to 45 days. During that window you can trade the reward but cannot withdraw it. Separately, rewards may be restricted from trading or withdrawal for up to 90 days from receipt, and unlocking them requires keeping your portfolio balance above a threshold for a cumulative 30 days inside that 90-day window; the threshold itself is shown in the app. Rewards must be claimed within 30 days of issuance, are distributed within 14 days after your task is verified, and crediting can take up to 48 hours. If nothing shows after 48 hours, support will ask for a screenshot of the completed task. 👉 [Track your lock-up and unlock date in Welcome Rewards](https://okx.com/join/CASH20)

### 4. The Hold Amount can freeze your own money

When you receive a crypto reward, OKX places a hold equal to the live market value of the reward quantity, and that hold moves with the price. The terms' own example: you receive 0.001 BTC when BTC is $100,000, so the reward is worth $100. You sell it for $100. BTC then rises to $120,000, the Hold Amount is recalculated at $120, and your $100 of available balance no longer covers it, so the whole balance shows as locked. The hold releases once the balance requirement is met and the reward unlocks.

The lesson is simple: selling a BTC-denominated reward does not free the cash. Keep enough unused balance to absorb a price move, or leave the reward alone until the unlock date.

## Is the €110 tier worth €100,000 of trading?

Run the fee arithmetic at OKX's advertised 0.1% spot floor:

- €500 of volume costs about €0.50 in fees and pays €10.
- €2,000 of volume costs about €2 and pays €20, which stacks with the previous €10 for €30.
- €10,000 of volume costs about €10 and pays €50.
- €100,000 of volume costs about €100 and pays €110.

The lower tiers pay multiples of their fee cost. The top tier pays roughly a 10% margin over fees, before spread, before slippage, and before whatever the market does to the non-stablecoin positions you had to hold to generate that volume. Add the requirement to keep €10,000 in net deposits on the platform for the duration and the €110 tier stops looking like a bonus and starts looking like a volume rebate you have to work for.
